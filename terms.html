COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

Vidyasutra Terms of Service

Welcome to Vidyasutra, a peer-to-peer campus skill exchange platform. By using our workspace, you agree to the following community principles:

1. Respectful Collaboration

Treat fellow learners and mentors with patience and respect. Offline study sessions should always take place in public campus areas such as libraries, cafeterias, or innovation labs.

2. Academic Integrity

Vidyasutra is designed for conceptual mentorship, code walkthroughs, and skill building — never for plagiarism or completing graded exams on someone else's behalf.

3. Fair Exchange

Honor agreed skill swaps, coffee treats, or mentorship schedules promptly. Communicate clearly if you need to reschedule.
