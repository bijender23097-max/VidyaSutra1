DATA & SECURITY

Vidyasutra Privacy Policy

Your privacy is built into the architecture of Vidyasutra:

1. Local-First Storage

In this standalone edition, your profile, roadmap milestones, community posts, and session requests are stored locally in your browser's localStorage and sessionStorage.

2. Password Hashing

Passwords are never stored in plain text. Vidyasutra derives a salted cryptographic hash using Web Crypto PBKDF2-SHA-256 (with a deterministic fallback for direct file protocol usage).

3. Full Control

You can update your profile or clear your browser storage at any time to reset your data.
